Claim the Land

Your people need a safe beginning. Move one step at a time: observe the World Board, learn the region types, choose one region, read the intel, record evidence, and defend the founding decision.

Step 1 — Observe the World Board

Before choosing land, look for what a nation needs: water, food, safety, movement, shelter, and risk. Do not decide yet.

Investigator Rule Observation comes before decision.
Your Job Notice what each region may offer and what danger it may bring.

Step 2 — Learn the Region Types

A region is a type of land. Each region gives people advantages, but each region also brings risks. Read these first. Then you will choose one region to investigate.

Step 3 — Choose One Region to Investigate

Select one region to investigate first. This is not the final founding decision. It only opens the evidence.

Choose one region to continue.
