Step 2 · Advisor Lenses

What kind of evidence matters?

Each advisor looks at the same evidence through a different lens. Use their lenses to think before you rank questions.

Scout Looks for visible changes: broken edges, new terrain clues, and details that were not there before.
Healer Looks for safety concerns: unstable ice, risky approach zones, and possible harm if the Council rushes.
Builder Looks for action limits: tools, access routes, structural risks, and what can be tested safely.
Mapkeeper Compares records: previous scan, current scan, old boundaries, and what changed over time.
