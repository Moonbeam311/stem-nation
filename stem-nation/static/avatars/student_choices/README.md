Purpose:

Students choose a personal Academy avatar before entering the STEM Nation Academy experience.

These are student avatar choices only.

They are not fixed Academy figures, advisors, Council members, mission roles, or teacher-facing identities.

Design requirements:

- realistic or semi-cinematic student portraits
- age-appropriate for middle-school / upper-elementary learners
- inclusive and diverse
- no text on image
- no logos
- no weapons
- no armor
- no fixed role symbols
- consistent warm gold / deep blue Academy atmosphere
