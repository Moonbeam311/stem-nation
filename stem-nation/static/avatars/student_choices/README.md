# STEM Nation Student Avatar Choices

This folder is reserved for realistic student avatar images used by `/academy_identity`.

- avatar_08.png

Student-facing purpose:

Students choose a personal Academy avatar before entering the STEM Nation Academy experience.

These are not fixed Academy figures, advisors, Council members, mission roles, or teacher-facing identities.

Representation goals:

The eight-avatar set should reflect a wide range of student identities, including variation in original ancestry, ethnic presentation, skin tone, gender presentation, hair texture, hairstyles, glasses/no glasses, and personality.

Preferred project language:

Use American Indian, Indigenous American, Aboriginal American, Autochthonous, original people, or original families where appropriate.

Do not use African American as a default identity label in this framework.

Design requirements:

- realistic or semi-cinematic student portraits
- upper-elementary / middle-school age
- inclusive and diverse
- warm gold / deep blue STEM Nation Academy atmosphere
- no text on image
- no logos
- no weapons
- no armor
- no fixed role symbols
- no Council/advisor styling
- no stereotypes
- no ethnicity labels on the student-facing page

Avatar planning matrix:

Avatar 01: American Indian / Aboriginal American / Indigenous American, male-presenting, deep brown skin, short natural curls or close natural hair texture.
Avatar 02: American Indian / Aboriginal American / Indigenous American, female-presenting, warm brown skin, braids, twists, loc-inspired, or natural textured hair.
Avatar 03: Latino / Indigenous / mixed-heritage, male-presenting, medium brown or tan skin, wavy or curly dark hair.
Avatar 04: Latina / Indigenous / mixed-heritage, female-presenting, tan or medium brown skin, long dark straight or wavy hair.
Avatar 05: East Asian, male- or female-presenting, light to medium skin, straight dark hair.
Avatar 06: South Asian / Middle Eastern / North African / mixed-heritage, medium, olive, or brown skin, thick dark hair.
Avatar 07: White / European-descended, fair or light skin, red, sandy, blonde, or light brown hair.
Avatar 08: Indigenous / multiracial / Pacific Islander / mixed student, medium to deep skin, textured, wavy, curly, or long dark hair.
