## Purpose of Each Inquiry Route

### /first_gathering
Council Hall entry.
Student enters mid-event.
Overheard disagreement.
Guardian approaches:

"Ah. You made it."

"We need another set of eyes."

"Come take a look."

Student sees unlabeled evidence before explanation.

### /academy_observation
Student Observation.
Evidence-before-explanation.
Student records three observations and one uncertainty.
The student is not asked to solve the mystery yet.

### /mystery_deepens
Guardian reveals that the images were taken six days apart.
The room has debated them all morning.
Student ranks exactly three questions by importance.

### /question_justification
Student explains why each ranked question matters.
The goal is reasoning, not a final answer.

### /advisor_reactions
Council advisors begin reacting to the kinds of questions the student prioritized.
Advisor identities are discovered through reactions, not introduced all at once.

### /mission_debrief
Student reflects on observation, question ranking, justification, and advisor reactions.
This locks the first inquiry trail before future transitions.

## Locked Design Principles

- The opening should feel like an adventure, not a lesson.
- Student begins as New Arrival / Academy Learner.
- Leadership is earned over time.
- Student avatar is separate from fixed Council advisors.
- Evidence appears before explanation.
- Guardian is the first direct Council contact.
- The mystery is introduced before it is explained.
- Question ranking comes before answer-making.
- Advisors are discovered through behavior and reaction.
- Mission Debrief / Reflection comes before any next major transition.
- No world selection transition is locked yet from mission debrief.
- Do not route this flow back to /baseline_inquiry.
- Do not use /academy_readiness unless intentionally reintroduced later.
